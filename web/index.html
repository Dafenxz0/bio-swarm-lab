Biological laboratory field guide

12 Differentiated Cell Types

  • Photocyte: Converts solar light intensity into ATP energy.
  • Phagocyte: Predatory jaws that engulf and lyse non-kin cells.
  • Flagellocyte: Undulating motile tail generating forward thrust.
  • Myocyte: Contractile muscle fiber flexing multicellular bond angles.
  • Neurocyte: High-plasticity pacemaker relaying synaptic pulses across bonds.
  • Senseocyte: Long-range optical and chemosensory receptor cone.
  • Immunocyte: Secretes neutralizing antibodies and acquires CRISPR strain memory.
  • Keratinocyte: Hexagonal armored epidermis deflecting predation and virions.
  • Secreocyte: Exocrine gland emitting pheromones, antibodies, toxins, or mucus.
  • Vascularocyte: Pumps ATP and metabolites across connected tissue bonds.
  • Stemocyte: Pluripotent progenitor driving rapid M0–M7 differentiation.
  • Scavenger: Digests cellular detritus and neutralizes ambient toxins.

Embryonic Modes, Brains & Virology

  • Multi-Mode Development (M0–M7): When a cell reaches its split mass, it divides along its configured split angle into Daughter A (child_a_mode) and Daughter B (child_b_mode), forming spring adhesion bonds.
  • Recurrent Hebbian Brain (12→10→8): Each cell runs a 12-sensor, 10-hidden, 8-effector neural network with 4 recurrent working-memory cells and online reward-modulated Hebbian weight adaptation.
  • Viral Strategies: Lytic phages replicate rapidly and burst the host; Lysogenic proviruses integrate into daughter cells during mitosis before activating; Oncogenic Mutators rewire host neural weights and accelerate genome drift.
